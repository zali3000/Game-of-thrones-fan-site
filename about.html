About the Chronicle

A fan-made interactive guide built around two eras of Westerosi history, keeping the original dark, cinematic style while making the experience easier to explore.

What you can explore

Browse houses, characters, dragons, cities and major events, then move between the later era of Game of Thrones and the earlier Dance of the Dragons.

FAQ

A few quick answers about the website.

Is this an official website?

No. It is an unofficial fan tribute using original presentation, writing and artwork.

Can I move between the two eras?

Yes. The Other Era controls in the navigation connect the Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon sections.

Is the site mobile friendly?

Yes. The navigation collapses on smaller screens and the grids and timelines adapt to mobile layouts.
