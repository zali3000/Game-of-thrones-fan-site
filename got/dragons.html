the only three dragons alive in a century

The Last Dragons

Three eggs, three names, one very short-lived era of the world remembering what dragons actually looked like. A hundred and seventy years earlier there were dozens — by this point, there were exactly three.

Before there were three

Dragons weren't always this rare. Under the early Targaryen kings, entire fleets of them shaped Westeros — several are still remembered by name, including the three that ended a war on their own during the Dance of the Dragons, a century and a half before this era.
